About

Finance leader and entrepreneur. Kellogg MBA. CPA, CA.

I’ve spent two decades working at the intersection of finance, technology, and operations — from auditing and investment banking in Mumbai, to corporate finance and global SAP transformation in Chicago, to franchise investing in early-childhood education.

At Interpublic Group (IPG), I serve as a Senior SAP Finance & Revenue Subject Matter Expert, leading a global team and partnering with agencies and networks across APAC, EMEA, and the Americas. Most of my work centers on revenue systems, process automation, and helping operators get clean, fast answers from complex ERP landscapes.

Outside of corporate work, I’m an investor and board member at The Goddard School of South Loop in downtown Chicago — an early-childhood education franchise that opened in 2020 and has grown to be the premier preschool in its market.

I started my career as an Audit Associate at Deloitte Haskins & Sells and a Credit Risk Monitoring Associate at ICICI Bank in Mumbai, then moved into project finance at SBI Capital Markets. After relocating to the U.S., I ran finance for TekPartner, a small-cap services company where I led the M&A integration that grew revenue from $1M to $4M, before joining IPG and earning my MBA from Kellogg.
